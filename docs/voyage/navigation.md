# Seas and navigation

Your first sail is simple. Pick a destination, follow its bearing, and step onto land with the crew. Once you know that loop, use the special channels to cross between seas.

## Log Pose and Atlas

1. **Open the One Piece World Log Pose on land.** It shows the current island, your discoveries, local missions, and destinations. If your next set of destinations is locked, finish the activities listed on the route screen.
2. **Set a destination.** Pick one from the island chart or destination list to put it on the compass. If the Red Line is in the way, the needle can point to a passage along the route.
3. **Sail the bearing.** Have the helmsman steer and one player watch weather and approach. Check the [Barometer forecast](../world/weather.md) before a long crossing. Use the [ship guide](shipbuilding.md) for helm and docking controls.
4. **Land and check in.** Step onto the island so the Log Pose registers the visit. Mark the shore or dock on your Atlas, then read the local objectives.

Your **Antique Atlas** automatically shares maps with your crewmates. Use `/surveyor share <player>` to share manually. A Sea Chart can mark treasure on the Atlas.

**Cruise’s Log Pose** is the Navigator’s tool for finding an island of a chosen kind. Crouch-use it to take a fresh bearing from where you stand. A Navigator can calibrate an **Eternal Pose** to the island they are on for a permanent bearing. Use the One Piece World Log Pose to check island mission and discovery credit.

## Crossing between seas

| If you are going… | Head for |
| --- | --- |
| From the Blues to Paradise | The uphill channel on your Blue’s side of [Reverse Mountain](paradise.md#cross-reverse-mountain). |
| From Paradise to the New World | [Sabaody and Fish-Man Island](new-world.md), with a coated ship and time to finish Ryugu Palace. |
| Into the Calm Belt | [Coat your ship](shipbuilding.md#kairoseki-coating) first, then follow the Log Pose to the [Gates of Justice](impel-down.md) or the [Gate of Hell](gates.md#gate-of-hell). |
| From Jaya to the sky | [Skypiea](special-destinations.md), via the Knock-Up Stream or Heaven’s Gate. |

## Returning to your ship

See [logging out on a moving ship](shipbuilding.md#logging-out-on-a-moving-ship) for stand-ins and emergency Drift Planks, or [bottling a ship](shipbuilding.md#bottle-your-ship) to carry it to another harbour.

## Fast travel

Visit both islands in person, claim **all three local mission rewards**, and spend the required time ashore. Pick a connected destination in the Log Pose’s **Fast travel** tab. The tab shows any missing residence, route, or ownership requirement.

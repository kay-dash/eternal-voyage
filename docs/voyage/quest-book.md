# The quest book

## The main chapters

| Chapter | What it follows |
| --- | --- |
| Romance Dawn | Character creation, professions, landfalls, Log Pose missions, and Higuma. |
| Villains of the Blues | Morgan, Kuro, Krieg, Arlong, and the route toward the Grand Line. |
| Paradise | Reverse Mountain, Alabasta, Jaya and the Knock-Up Stream, Water 7, Enies Lobby, and Thriller Bark. |
| Summit War | Sabaody, Amazon Lily, Impel Down, and Marineford, with a Gate of Hell objective. |
| New World | Fish-Man Island, Mariejois, Punk Hazard, Dressrosa, Zou, Whole Cake Island, Wano, and the Road Poneglyphs. |
| Final Saga | Elbaph, Laugh Tale, and The One Piece. |
| World Events | Buster Call: Enies Lobby and The Battle of Sabaody. |

The book also has **Factions**, **Fighting Styles**, **Races**, **Professions**, **Bounty Board**, and **Endgame**. Quest progress is personal, even when your crew fights or sails together. A chapter can lock if you change factions, races, styles, or professions. Completed quests stay completed.

The [quest chapter reference](../reference/quests/index.md) records every supplied chapter’s objectives, prerequisites and rewards, including the rotating Bounty Board and Pirate King’s Legacy.

The [Bounty Board](../reference/quests/bounty_board.md) mixes daily and weekly contracts. Check the active period before saving work for later. Weekly hot zones cycle through Marineford, Sabaody, Enies Lobby, Wano, and Fish-Man Island. For FTB PvP objectives, the script excludes victims below **2,000 Doriki** and applies a **30-minute** repeat-victim cooldown.

When a quest reward would exceed your Doriki cap, unused Doriki converts to Belly at **8 Belly** per point. A reward for an already maxed Haki type converts at **400 Belly** per Haki point. These are the quest runtime’s reward rules.

Some objectives track server-wide accomplishments alongside your own. For example, **Raid on Onigashima** lists 1,000 server victories in Wano, 30 personal victories, and 37,500 Doriki. Read all of a quest’s tasks before grinding a single counter. When a faction finale offers a fruit choice, use the chat menu or `/fruitreward` to choose.

Great Haki Realm events are configured for admin starts. Check with your server when a world-event objective is waiting on an event.

**Pack sources:** `config/ftbquests/quests/`, `kubejs/server_scripts/eternal_voyage_quests.js`, and `hakirework-server.toml`.

# Skypiea

## Sail from Jaya { #skypiea }

Follow the [Paradise chapter](../reference/quests/03_paradise.md) through **Jaya**, the **Knock-Up Stream**, and **God Enel**. Bring a **balloon octopus** for the descent. At Jaya, find the Knock-Up Stream and move into it during an eruption. A compatible assembled ship can ride the stream with its crew. Heaven’s Gate is the other route up.

| Area | What to find |
| --- | --- |
| Angel Islands | Skypiean villages, Heaven’s Gate, Angel Beach, the White Berets, and routes on the cloud sea. |
| Upper Yards | Shandia camps, Shandora, the Giant Jack, the Golden Bell, and the priest and Enel ordeals. |
| Birka | Scarred islands, surviving settlements, and ruins. |
| Weatheria | Weather scientists, Haredas, and weather research. The [Art of Weather](../reference/quests/st_art_of_weather.md) chapter takes this route. Weather calls connect to the pack’s [local weather](../world/weather.md). |

Use a Waver to travel along Milky Roads. On the way down, use a balloon octopus on a ship block to soften the landing. Check the [Sky Island content catalog](../reference/content/inosky.md) for dials, wildlife, blocks and items. [Sky Island guide](https://inovactio.github.io/mods/sky-island/)

Skypiea uses the Late Paradise tier for eligible regional encounters. Shandia Warriors, Dial Warriors and White Beret Guards use **5×** their original Doriki and Haki, with Haki capped at **500** per type. Their HP follows the pack’s Doriki curve and their damage gets a **2×** multiplier. The [challenge table](../reference/settings/encounters.md#challenge-bosses) lists the separate stages for Satori, Shura, Gedatsu, Ohm and Enel.

Continue the main voyage from [Paradise](paradise.md#pick-your-next-boss) or prepare for the [undersea crossing](new-world.md).

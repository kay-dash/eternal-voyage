# Impel Down

## Get to the prison { #impel-down }

To visit on purpose, take a [Kairoseki-coated ship](shipbuilding.md#kairoseki-coating) into the **Calm Belt** and find the **Gates of Justice**. The prison’s great gate opens into Level 0. Stay clear of the guards until you’re ready to fight. The **Summit War** quest asks for **25,000 Doriki** and a trip to **Level 6, Eternal Hell**.

The **Gates of Justice** lead to the prison. Use the [Gate of Hell](gates.md#gate-of-hell) for the Nether route.

## How to arrest a player

As a **Marine** or **Bounty Hunter**, defeat an eligible **Pirate** or **Revolutionary Army** player in combat. If the arrest applies, the finishing blow sends them to a nearby prison instead of killing them. A prison has to be in range. You can also be arrested by Marine NPCs, Pacifistas, and prison guards. A guard who arrests you inside a tower can send you to a cell on that floor. Bounty normally decides which floor you get outside the tower. Giants can be sent straight to a Giant cell on Level 6.

| Bounty at arrest | Floor |
| ---: | ---: |
| Below 15,000 | 1 |
| 15,000–49,999 | 2 |
| 50,000–74,999 | 3 |
| 75,000–99,999 | 4 |
| 100,000–249,999 | 5 |
| 250,000 or more | 6 |

An arrested player’s gear goes to the private **depot chest** on their floor. While the cell is shut, Kairoseki cuffs prevent ability use and trying to get out by another route can put them back in the cell.

## Get out of your cell

| Route | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Snatch the jailer’s key | Watch the bars. A jailer passes on rounds roughly every **three minutes**. Land a hit through the bars to take the key. Use it on your floor’s cell door. |
| Follow a riot | Around every **20 minutes** of prison time, a riot can open cells on your floor and raise the alarm. Use the opening to get moving. |
| Search the cell | If you find a hidden hook, hold it by the lock for **three minutes** to force the door. |
| Have a crewmate help | A crew can open the door from outside with the right Cell Key or use a Master Key at the Control Desk. Lift Keys can help with the route between floors. |
| Serve your sentence | Wait out **two minutes plus one minute per 5,000 bounty**, up to **30 minutes online**. Check the time in your action bar. On release, the prison returns your gear and sends you back to the arrest location. |

## Escape through the great gate

1. Once the cell opens, your abilities return and the guards can pursue you. Use the direction given in chat to find your **floor’s depot** and get your gear.
2. Work up toward **Level 0**. A Cell Key opens doors on its own floor. A **Lift Key** lets you use a Lift Gate or Lift Desk. A **Master Key** opens cells on a floor from its Control Desk.
3. Go out through the **great gate on Level 0** to register an escape and earn the escape bounty. Getting caught or dying in the tower can send you back to your cell. Have your crew ready to pick you up outside.

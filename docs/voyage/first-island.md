# Your first island

Your first stop is where you learn the two loops you’ll repeat throughout the voyage. The **quest book** tracks story milestones and your character’s training. The **One Piece World Log Pose** shows the current island’s jobs and records your visits.

## Make camp and pick a first job

1. Open **Romance Dawn** in the quest book and claim the **Rookie Crate**. Equip what fits your fighting style and keep food, blocks, and tools handy.
2. Pick a safe meeting point near shore. Set a bed where you are allowed to build and mark your landing spot on the Atlas. Leave a clear path down to the water.
3. Step onto the island and open your Log Pose. Choose an active local job. Read its target and collect its reward from the Log Pose after you finish. **The Log Pose Remembers** counts all three collected rewards from the same island.
4. Start doing the work your trades reward. Gather, craft, or hunt for the supplies you already need and check your trade chapter as you go.

If your island has a town, stop by its notice board or shops before taking on a camp. If you land on a ruins island, use its local objective to find out whether clearing it calls for a boss or its defenders.

## Fight some bandits

The **Mountain Bandits** quest asks you to defeat **ten bandits**. Bring spare food, clear a way back to shore, and let a crewmate handle snipers while the front line takes on groups. Open **Challenges** for **Higuma the Bear** after the bandit objective. Claim the chapter rewards yourself even if your crew went through the same steps.

## Find two more islands

**Set Sail** tracks **three different islands**. Land on each one to register it and open the Log Pose after landing. A small boat is enough to scout a nearby shore. For repeat trips with several players and gear, [build and test a crew ship](shipbuilding.md), then [choose your next landfall](navigation.md).

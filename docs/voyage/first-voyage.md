# Getting started

Launch the **Eternal Voyage** profile for **Minecraft Java 1.20.1 Forge**. Open **Multiplayer**, add the Eternal Voyage server using the address provided by the server team, and join. Your first voyage starts in the Blues. You’ll take island jobs, earn Doriki, outfit a ship, and build a crew before sailing into tougher seas. The quest book gives you a route through the story and separate goals for your race, fighting style, and trades.

## Make your character

1. Choose a [race](../characters/races/index.md), [faction](../characters/factions-and-trades.md#factions), and [fighting style](../characters/fighting-styles.md). Race affects your body and racial techniques. Your style determines how you fight and which weapons or equipment to carry. Your faction gives you different allies, ranks, and objectives.
2. Open the quest book and claim **Romance Dawn** for the Rookie Crate, 90 Doriki, 3,000 Belly, and five XP levels.
3. **Choose two professions.** The first choice completes **A Dream on the Horizon**. Reach level 1 in that trade to open your second slot. Use the [profession guide](../characters/factions-and-trades.md#professions) to choose work you’ll do regularly and cover different jobs across your crew.

| If you want to… | A pair to start with |
| --- | --- |
| Keep a ship running | **Carpenter + Navigator** for hull work, charts, and weather checks |
| Supply the crew | **Farmer + Cook** for steady food and meals |
| Make expedition gear | **Miner + Blacksmith** for materials and weapons |
| Handle remedies and devices | **Chemist + Inventor** for mixtures and dials |

## Form up before sailing

Create a crew with `/crew create <name>` or accept the captain’s invitation. Use local chat to talk with players nearby when you meet in port. Ask who will helm and watch the weather, who will handle repairs and supplies, and who will take point during fights. A smaller crew can share those jobs. Maps are shared with crewmates. Quest progress is personal, so each player should check their objectives and claim rewards.

To start together, invite a friend with `/starter invite <player>`. The invitation lasts **15 minutes**. They can click the chat offer or run `/starter with <player>` while still on their starter island. Crewmates can use the latter command with their captain. After leaving your own starter island, the crew’s starter island can serve as your respawn point when you have no bed.

## Pack for a landing

Everyone should carry food, armour, a way back to the ship, and the weapon or tool their moves require. Carry a spare boat for landing and keep a way to check the [local weather](../world/weather.md). Agree where to regroup if the crew gets separated.

| Role | What to bring |
| --- | --- |
| Front line | Armour, food, and the weapon that matches your style. A Brawler can keep an empty slot ready for bare-handed moves. |
| Ranged support | A suitable firearm and ammunition, or a Clima-Tact for Art of Weather. Carry a backup for close quarters. |
| Ship and shore support | Navigation tools, extra supplies, and hull-repair materials if you’re the Carpenter. A Doctor can bring healing tools while another player watches for threats. |

Decide together before eating a [Devil Fruit](../characters/devil-fruits.md). It gives a new moveset and makes swimming dangerous. A fruitless crew can keep stronger late-game combat bonuses if everyone commits to it.

## Your first session

Your crew’s first goal is to stock up and complete a few jobs on the island, then head to another port together. Open [Your first island](first-island.md) for the Rookie Crate, Log Pose, and first fight.

## Before you leave the Blues

- Sail to three different islands for **Set Sail**. Land on each island to record it in your navigation log.
- On an island, open its Log Pose and claim all three local mission rewards for **The Log Pose Remembers**.
- Defeat ten bandits for **Mountain Bandits**, then take on **Higuma the Bear**. Follow the [Blues chapters](quest-book.md) for the next fights and the path to Reverse Mountain.
- Restock before the crossing. Make sure every crewmate knows which channel you’re taking and where to meet in Paradise.

Continue with [Your first island](first-island.md), or jump to [Reverse Mountain](paradise.md#cross-reverse-mountain) if your crew is ready to leave.

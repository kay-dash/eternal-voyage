# Reach the Grand Line

## Cross Reverse Mountain

1. In the Blues story, defeat any four of the listed challenge bosses and finish **Strongest of Your Blue**. The **Reverse Mountain** quest checks **2,500 Doriki** and entry into the Grand Line. The sailing passage itself is a current that carries boats and ships.
2. Set the Log Pose toward **Reverse Mountain** and approach the **uphill channel on your own Blue’s side** of the mountain. Use a boat or a compact, floating Eureka ship that fits between the banks. Bring food for the crossing and expect hostile mobs in the channels.
3. Put the bow into the **middle of the uphill water**. Release the anchor and follow the current instead of steering toward a bank. At the summit, take the **downhill channel into Paradise**.
4. Find a landing and put it on your Atlas. Open the quest book to claim the Reverse Mountain reward and see the next objectives.

## Unlock and train Haki

Entering **Paradise** is what opens Haki training. Kuja awaken Armament and Observation on arrival. Three-Eye characters awaken Observation. Other characters can pursue combat awakening or visit a mentor. Start with the kind that suits your role. Armament helps the front line meet harder hits and reach Logia bodies. Observation helps a scout read threats or an agile fighter avoid blows.

Use the [Haki guide](../characters/haki.md#awaken-and-train-haki) to choose an awakening method. After awakening, read the [Haki trees](../characters/haki-tree.md) before spending mastery on a fork. If you fight with swords or martial arts, check the [school requirements](../characters/schools.md) and find the appropriate master.

## Pick your next boss

| Where you are | What to tackle |
| --- | --- |
| Early Paradise | Follow the Whiskey Peak, Little Garden and Drum objectives, then head toward Alabasta and Crocodile. Use the local jobs to build Doriki between challenges. |
| Jaya | Prepare the ship for the Knock-Up Stream. The optional [Skypiea route](special-destinations.md) takes you through the Upper Yard and Enel. |
| Late Paradise | Continue toward Water 7, Enies Lobby and Thriller Bark. Check the island mission before chasing a boss from a different quest or arena. |
| Sabaody and Summit War | Use Sabaody as a supply and coating stop. The [Summit War chapter](../reference/quests/04_summit_war.md) takes you through the prison and Marineford objectives. |

Before a challenge, check the [encounter stats](../world/encounter-stats.md), set your techniques, and carry the gear those moves need. Read the objective for whether it expects an arena win, an Overworld fight, or a local Log Pose mission. Hard rematches are a late-game goal.

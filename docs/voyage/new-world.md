# Reach the New World

The undersea route takes you from late Paradise to **Fish-Man Island and the Ryugu Kingdom**, then up to the New World. Do the three palace missions while you’re there so your Log Pose reveals the onward route.

## Prepare at Sabaody

1. Bring a compact, **assembled** Eureka ship. The undersea route takes ships up to **56 blocks corner to corner** and **46 blocks across**. Trim overhangs before leaving if your hull is close to the limit.
2. Obtain **Dense Kairoseki** and [fully coat the helm](shipbuilding.md#kairoseki-coating). Check for the completed-coating message before you leave.
3. Bring personal breathing supplies too. The coating yard at **Sabaody** sells a **Coated Bubble** for **5,000 Belly**. At Ryugu Palace, a **Ryugu Bubble** lasts **15 minutes**. See [fruit users and bubbles](../world/survival.md#fruit-users-and-bubbles), especially if anyone in your crew has eaten a Devil Fruit.
4. Set a Log Pose heading for **Fish-Man Island**. Follow the needle from Paradise to the **undersea passage on the Red Line**.

## Let the current take you down

Bring the ship to the centre of the passage and sail into the **undersea current**. The ship must be coated and must fit the channel. Stay aboard as the current carries you to Fish-Man Island’s dock. If the ship is refused, check the message for hull size or coating. Dock with room to turn the ship around and have everyone step ashore to register the visit.

## Open the route at Ryugu Palace

1. Follow the local Log Pose job to **Ryugu Palace**.
2. Beat **Arlong** and deal with his guards near the palace. Check the active local mission before leaving the area.
3. Return to the palace and collect the third reward to reveal the **New World** route. The FTB **Fish-Man Island** quest also checks all three collected missions.

If your FTB quest is still locked, finish **Two Years Later** in the Summit War chapter. Keep progress on the local missions and the story quest in view as you prepare to leave. [Fish-Man Island quest](../reference/quests/05_new_world.md#q-490fded851490c64).

## Ride the hot stream to the surface

Return to the harbour. Head for the **hot stream in Fish-Man Island’s bay** and steer into its centre to ride it **up to the New World**. The return current at the other end of the dock runs **back to Paradise**. Watch for the current’s message so the crew knows which way the ship is headed. Stay aboard until you’re back on the surface.

## Make your first New World landfall

Land together, mark the dock, and check local storm conditions before going back out. The next story route includes **Punk Hazard**, **Dressrosa**, **Zou**, **Whole Cake Island**, and **Wano**. Keep a crew record of your Road Poneglyph readings as the quest book starts to ask for them. New World opponents are stronger and storms can reach hurricane strength.

# Build and captain a ship

Use a regular boat for scouting or short landings. Build an **Eureka! ship** when your crew needs room for people, cargo, and a long crossing. A block-built hull is the ship you can coat, bottle, and leave a stand-in aboard.

## Build and assemble

1. Build a small wooden hull near the shoreline. Keep the hull separate from the dock and any temporary supports. Eureka assembles connected blocks, so remove any block bridge to the shore before you press **Assemble**.
2. Craft a **Ship Helm** with **4 sticks, 3 matching wooden fences, 1 matching slab, and 1 gold ingot**. Place the helm facing the bow with space to stand at it.
3. Add buoyancy with **Floaters** if the hull sits too low. One plank surrounded by four string makes **16 Floaters**. Spread them across the hull to keep it level. Keep heavy cargo and fittings balanced.
4. With an empty hand, **sneak-right-click** the helm and choose **Assemble**. Once it floats, right-click the helm normally to take control. Start with a short run where you can check steering, freeboard, and turning room before loading everyone.

The [Eureka Helm guide](https://valkyrienskies.org/wiki/Ship_Helm) shows the menu and vanilla control defaults.

## Outfit and test your ship

| Fit the ship for… | What to add |
| --- | --- |
| Crew travel | A clear deck, rails, a place to get in from water, and supplies where the crew can find them. |
| Heavy hulls | More Floaters if it rides too low. Use Ballast when you need to lower it. |
| Stopping and docking | An **Eureka Anchor** for keeping the ship in place. Practise stopping in open water before approaching a dock or riding a current. |
| Frozen water | Fit a **Valkyrien Icebreakers Ice Breaker** at the bow for surface ice. Craft it with one iron block and four iron bars. |
| Calm Belt or Fish-Man Island | Fully coat the assembled ship. See [Kairoseki coating](#kairoseki-coating) below. |

Use **W/S** to go ahead or astern and **A/D** to turn. **Sneak** leaves the helm. Check the in-game keybinds if your controls differ. **C** toggles cruise control while you pilot. Turn it off and bring the ship to a stop before people step ashore. To turn the ship back into ordinary blocks, stop in clear water, then use **Align** and **Disassemble** in the helm menu.

A Carpenter can also build and upgrade **Cruise boats**. Their hull plating, repair and sail upgrades belong to that boat system. See [Carpenter](../characters/factions-and-trades.md#professions) and [storm handling](../world/weather.md#read-the-weather-and-protect-your-ship).

## Kairoseki coating

Mine **Kairoseki Ore** or trade for Kairoseki. Craft **four Kairoseki** in a 2×2 square for one **Dense Kairoseki**.

**Assemble first. Hold Dense Kairoseki and right-click the Ship Helm.** The helm takes as much as it needs from the stack and reports your progress. Budget **one piece per 40 hull blocks**, rounded up, with a **minimum of 5 and a maximum of 64**. Use the helm’s completion message to confirm the coat before sailing.

The coating lets your ship cross the **Calm Belt** and take the **undersea route to Fish-Man Island**. It hides the ship from Sea Kings there. An uncoated block-built ship can have its hull torn apart in the Calm Belt. A **Coated Bubble** protects a person underwater. Apply **Dense Kairoseki** separately to prepare the ship.

If you change or redeploy a hull before a crossing, check the coating at the helm again. Dense Kairoseki works on supported small boats too. Apply it to the boat until it reaches full coating, which takes five applications on ordinary boats.

## Logging out on a moving ship

Stand on the assembled ship before disconnecting. The Companion leaves a **stand-in** that marks your spot aboard while the crew sails. When you log in, it returns you to the ship where it is now if it can still find it.

If the ship is gone, you may come back adrift. **Keep walking toward land or another ship.** Temporary **Drift Planks** appear under you as you move. Each lasts **30 seconds**, and you can have up to **64** active at once. Have a crewmate meet you if the nearest shore is too far. Ask everyone to get off before bottling the ship.

## Bottle your ship

1. Craft a **Big Bottle** from **7 glass blocks and 1 Heart of the Sea**. Put glass across the top and bottom rows and in the middle-right slot, with the Heart of the Sea in the centre.
2. Stop the assembled ship and get everyone ashore. Hold the Big Bottle and **right-click a ship block while standing normally**. The bottle takes **30 experience levels** in Survival.
3. Carry the **Ship In A Bottle** to its destination. **Right-click open water without sneaking** to launch it. Leave enough clear space in and above the water for the full hull. Set the bottle down while sneaking if you want it as a display.
4. Check the helm and confirm any coating before attempting a gated crossing.

[Ship In A Bottle’s guide](https://modrinth.com/mod/vs-ship-in-a-bottle) covers the bottle and launch behavior.

## Naming and retrieving ships

Use a **Ship Namer** on the assembled hull so you can identify it later. At an equipped harbour, a **Hub Controller** manages docking slots. To pair your ship, right-click its mounted **Ship Anchor** with a **Hub Linker**, then right-click the harbour’s **Hub Extension** for the slot. When you have permission, call the ship back from the paired dock. Keep the landing space clear. The Ship Anchor for a retrieval hub is a different block from the Eureka Anchor you use to hold a ship in place.

[Ship Retrieval Service’s guide](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vs-ship-retrieval-service) explains the harbour equipment.

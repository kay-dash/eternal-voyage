# The Voyage

Start here if this is your first time on Eternal Voyage. Follow the main route from the Blues to the New World and come back for side trips when your crew is ready. The quest book gives you objectives to claim along the way. The Log Pose tells you where to go.

| Stage | What to do next |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting Started](first-voyage.md) | Join the server, create a character, choose two professions, and meet your crew. |
| [Your first island](first-island.md) | Claim the Rookie Crate, make landfall, finish local jobs, and take on Higuma. |
| [Build and captain a ship](shipbuilding.md) | Assemble a hull, learn the helm, and practise docking. Learn how to bottle a ship or safely leave one behind. |
| [Sail to the next island](navigation.md) | Choose a Log Pose heading, share maps, and land together. |
| [Grow stronger in the Blues](progression.md) | Earn Doriki, level your trades, fight the first bosses, and advance your faction. |
| [Towns and island claims](../world/conflict.md) | Make friends in port or take and defend a home island for your crew. |
| [Reach the Grand Line](paradise.md) | Ride Reverse Mountain into Paradise and start training Haki. |
| [Skypiea](special-destinations.md) | Make the optional trip from Jaya into the sky, then take on the Upper Yard trials. |
| [Reach the New World](new-world.md) | Coat a ship at Sabaody, sail under the Red Line to the Ryugu Kingdom, and ride the hot stream to the far side. |
| [Gate of Hell and the final voyage](gates.md) | Open the Nether branch when eligible, then follow the Road Poneglyphs to Laugh Tale. |

**If you’re under arrest or heading to the prison**, see [Impel Down](impel-down.md) for captures, sentences, and the escape route. **If you’re stuck on an objective**, use [The quest book](quest-book.md) to find the chapter and its prerequisites.

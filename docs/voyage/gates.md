# Gate of Hell and the final voyage

Take these routes when your character meets their requirements. The Gate of Hell is a side branch you can take once eligible. Laugh Tale is the end of the main voyage.

## Gate of Hell

Take a [coated ship](shipbuilding.md#kairoseki-coating) through the Calm Belt and set the Log Pose to the **Gate of Hell**. Land at the ruined portal and follow the **local Log Pose mission**. Reach the portal area and defeat **three waves** of guardians, then collect the reward that opens the gate.

To enter the Nether through the gate, reach **12,500 Doriki** and **Initiate** rank in either **Armament** or **Observation**. Each player should check their own readiness and bring gear for the return voyage. The FTB chapter has its own [Gate of Hell objective](../reference/quests/04_summit_war.md#the-gate-of-hell).

## Red Line and Mary Geoise

To stand atop the Red Line at **Y 181** or higher or cross the world edge at Mary Geoise, you need **Proficient Armament**. The Mariejois local mission starts on the plateau and then asks you to defeat a Marine vice admiral. For the route under the Red Line, follow [Fish-Man Island](new-world.md).

## Laugh Tale and the End

Gather **four Road Poneglyph readings** across yourself and your crew. Follow the [New World chapter](../reference/quests/05_new_world.md#the-road-poneglyphs) to the stones, then [Final Saga](../reference/quests/06_final_saga.md) to Elbaph and Laugh Tale.

The gate to the End checks **50,000 Doriki**, **Master** rank in both Armament and Observation, and the four readings. The FTB Laugh Tale objective also asks for maxed Armament and Observation. Check both the gate requirements and the quest tasks before you depart.

## Other landmark missions

At **Sabaody**, reach the landing, beat the notorious pirate captain, and clear the bandit den. **Enies Lobby** follows landfall with a Marine vice admiral. **Marineford** follows a three-wave challenge with Akainu. **Wano** sends you to a mine den before Kaido. **Hachinosu** sends you to a captain at the skull base before Blackbeard.

For the prison and its great gate, see [Impel Down](impel-down.md).

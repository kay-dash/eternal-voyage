# Pack rules

Look up the rules for playing Eternal Voyage, including combat scaling, loot, and travel. Browse the [mod guides](content/index.md) for each mod’s features.

| System | Eternal Voyage rule | Where to read more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The seas | Start in one of the four Blues and sail through Paradise into the New World. Islands and encounters change as you progress. | [Seas and navigation](../voyage/navigation.md) |
| Doriki, health and damage | Base health reaches 500 HP at 50,000 Doriki. Damage scales by category above 10,000 Doriki, with separate factors for individual moves. | [Combat stats and Doriki scaling](../characters/combat-stats.md) |
| Haki crews | An entirely fruitless crew with three or more members reaches a 2.75× Haki and fighting-style multiplier at the cap. | [Fruitless crew scaling](../characters/haki.md#fruitless-crew-scaling) |
| Haki techniques and schools | Use the Haki tree for mastery unlocks, or learn from a master for a sword school or martial art. | [Haki tree](../characters/haki-tree.md) and [Schools](../characters/schools.md) |
| Races and inherited traits | Your race and variant shape your body and available moves. Lineage Factors can add racial traits. | [Races and lineage](../characters/races-and-styles.md) |
| Professions | Choose two professions. Your second starts at level 1. | [Factions and professions](../characters/factions-and-trades.md#professions) |
| Devil Fruits | Find fruits through box pools and other rewards. Uneaten fruits last seven real days. | [Devil Fruits and awakenings](../characters/devil-fruits.md) |
| Fruit awakening | Reach 40,000 Doriki and build active online time with a fruit that can awaken. | [Fruit awakening](../characters/devil-fruits.md#awakening) |
| Sea storms | Storms get stronger farther into the voyage. Check the Barometer and prepare your ship. | [Survival at sea](../world/survival.md#weather-and-temperature) |
| Sea Kings | Hunt Sea Kings in the Calm Belt or fish for them at the required Fisher level and depth. | [Loot and encounters](../world/loot.md) |
| Encounters and treasure | Crews, toughness and damage vary by sea. Arena opponents and loot also depend on tier. | [Encounter stats](../world/encounter-stats.md) and [Loot](../world/loot.md) |
| Conquest | Towns use village flags. Other claimable islands use halls, assaults, defenses and crew wars. | [Towns, crews and conflict](../world/conflict.md) |

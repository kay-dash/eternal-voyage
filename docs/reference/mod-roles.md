# World Economy News

The [Eternal Voyage Companion](content/eternalvoyage.md) adds the **Press Card** for World Economy News. The card works with the Camera Den Den Mushi.

## Craft a Press Card

Combine **two paper**, **one ink sac**, **one feather**, and **one gold nugget**. The recipe is shapeless.

## Publish and interview

- Hold a signed book and use `/press print`. Put a photograph in your offhand to use it on the front page.
- Use `/press extra` for the extra edition feature.
- Use `/press interview <player>` to start an interview.

See [Transponder Snails](content/transpondersnails.md) for the mod’s communication items.

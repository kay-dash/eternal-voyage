# Schools and martial arts

## Join and learn

Find a master for the school or art and **sneak-interact**. Complete the master’s sparring objective, using the appropriate weapons or fighting posture. Standard lessons unlock as you meet the Doriki and Haki requirements. For a finisher, return to a master and complete the lesson with the required Haki active when the trial asks for it.

Swordsmen and **Nitoryu** fighters can attend sword schools in the shipped build. The standard sword-school finisher setting is **15,000 Doriki**, with school-specific gates in the quest lessons. Switching sword schools has a **60-real-minute** cooldown. The Haki numbers below are how the book presents the lesson requirements. Check the master’s trial and your current Haki before attempting a finisher.

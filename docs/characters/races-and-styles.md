# Races and lineage

Your race changes how you move, fight, and survive. Choose the body and moveset you want in the character creator, then check the race page before choosing a variant. Each page lists the moves, passives, and racial Lineage Factors together, with related quests at the end.

| If you want to… | Start with | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fight underwater | [Fish-Man](races/fishman.md), [Merfolk](races/merfolk.md), [Wotan](races/wotan.md) | Fishman Karate and aquatic movement, with different bodies, reach, and passives. Wotans can also use Giant techniques. |
| Fight with a larger body | [Giant](races/giant.md), [Buccaneer](races/buccaneer.md), [Oni](races/oni.md) | Compare reach, power, mobility, and the space each body needs to fight. |

| Learn body techniques or use equipment | [Human](races/human.md), [Cyborg](races/cyborg.md) | Train Rokushiki, choose a Germa variant, or fit Cola-powered equipment to change your moveset. |
| Build around innate techniques | [Long-Arm](races/long_arm.md), [Long-Leg](races/long_leg.md), [Kuja](races/kuja.md), [Three-Eye](races/three_eye.md) | Specialize in long-reaching strikes, kicks, snake arrows, or the third eye. |

## Race, Lineage Factors and bloodlines

Your **race and variant** determine your body and racial moveset. A **Lineage Factor** gives you the effect listed on the graft, such as underwater strength or heat protection. Check the race page for associated factors and their limits. **Bloodlines** such as Will of D. and the Kozuki Clan are inherited traits with their own rules. See the [bloodline catalog](bloodlines/index.md#bloodline-catalog) for the complete list.

Choose a [fighting style](fighting-styles.md) that complements your race. A Fish-Man can fight underwater while the rest of the crew stays aboard, and a Cyborg should include Cola in their loadout. Size can affect reach and damage, so consider how much room you have on a ship or in a dungeon. See [combat stats](combat-stats.md#defense-size-and-logia).

# Willpower

![](../assets/playable/hakirework/abilities/willpower.png){ .entry-page-icon }

Willpower is its own progression under Haki Realm. Spending Haki, fighting on with an empty Haki bar, surviving severe damage, and beating stronger opponents build it. Standing up to Conqueror’s Haki can also award Willpower. The pack uses the normal **1.00×** gain rate.

Each of the ten levels opens a passive in the **Willpower** tab of the ability menu. Open `/haki willpower` to check progress and see the next passive.

| Level | Total Willpower XP | Passive | Effect |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 | Steady Breath | Haki refill speed +10%. |
| 2 | 300 | Deep Reserves | Refunds 8% of the Haki you spend. |
| 3 | 600 | Grit | Haki refill speed +50% while below one-third health. |
| 4 | 1,000 | Unbowed | Conqueror’s Haki knockouts last one-third less time. |
| 5 | 1,500 | Greater Will | Haki capacity ×1.15. |
| 6 | 2,100 | Refuse to Fall | Survive an eligible killing blow at 1 HP with an empty Haki bar. Cooldown 10 minutes. |
| 7 | 2,800 | Burning Will | Adds 15 percentage points to Haki refill speed and 5 percentage points to Haki cost refunds. |
| 8 | 3,600 | Indomitable | Take 15% less damage from Conqueror’s-coated hits and techniques. |
| 9 | 4,500 | Last Stand | Deal 15% more damage while below one-quarter health. |
| 10 | 5,500 | Unbreakable Will | Adds 20 percentage points to Haki refill speed and multiplies capacity by another 1.15. |

See [Doriki and Haki](haki.md) for the Haki bar, mastery branches, and combat training.

# Fighting styles

Choose a fighting style in the character creator. Style trainers and relevant FTB quests award Training Points. The supplied base Training Point limit is **150**, and the configured retention after a reset is **80%**. Race points and style points follow the trees and reward types shown in the game. A quest that asks you to unlock a move checks the unlock itself.

Open the [fighting style catalogue](styles/index.md) to choose a style, or select a style or weapon art below for its moves, unlocks, and stats. Check the [quest chapter reference](../reference/quests/index.md) for the objectives you can complete as you train.

## Blades, Wills and Haki

Choose a separate weapon for each of the three paths. A blade can hold one of a curse, Black Blade status, or a Zoan infusion. Black Fists are trained on your character.

### Cursed blades · Black Haki

Carry a cursed sword and **sneak-use** it to take the Test of Fate. If accepted, the sword names you as its bearer. Luck improves your chances. A failed test hurts and imposes a waiting period. Once you are accepted, fight strong enemies with that blade to feed its curse and reach levels II and III.

Most new curses require at least **3,000 Doriki** and a sword with **7 attack damage**. Named swords such as Sandai Kitetsu and Enma have their own curses. Other swords awaken through specific deeds. There are ten curses, and available places for new curses are limited. The [curse origins](../reference/quests/special_weapons.md#q-39ac9dad9f917023) describe how to seek each one.

### Black Blades and Black Fists · Black Haki

- **Black Blade.** Coat an eligible blade with **Armament Imbuing** and land fully charged hits against strong enemies. Repeated hits on the same target give diminishing progress. When the blade is at least halfway to blackening and you have **70 Armament XP**, take it to a **Kokuto Forge** with a **Kokuto Whetstone**. Stay near the forge with Imbuing active and avoid taking damage during the ritual. After blackening, fight with that blade and return to the forge for later rank trials.
- **Black Fists.** Choose **Brawler** or **Black Leg**, activate **Armament Hardening**, and land qualifying bare-handed hits with an empty main hand. This path has four ranks and can improve what you mine with hardened fists as well as how you fight.

### Zoan weapons · Infusion

Look for the **Zoan Affinity** tooltip on your weapon and choose a supported Zoan. Drop the weapon and the uneaten fruit together to bind them. Binding consumes the fruit and the weapon’s tooltip shows which fruit it carries. Its available techniques depend on the fruit. Some turn the weapon into a companion with its own moves. A fruit bound to a weapon is safe from the rot timer.

Open the [Blades, Wills and Haki quests](../reference/quests/special_weapons.md) for rank checks, curse deeds, and the infused-weapon trial on Sabaody.

The [style damage curve](combat-stats.md#damage-from-doriki) reaches **1.50×** at 50,000 Doriki for a fruit user, **2.50×** for a fruitless character, and **2.75×** for an eligible all-fruitless crew. Individual moves may also have [pack damage multipliers](../reference/settings/move-damage.md). Check [Schools and martial arts](schools.md) for moves that require a master.

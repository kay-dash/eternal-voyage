# Farmer

![](../../assets/playable/inocruise/gui/profession/farmer.png){ .entry-page-icon }

[All professions](index.md)

Collect your first produce from wild plants. Sow or plant it where the soil matches, wait until it ripens, and harvest it yourself for trade XP. Wild crops can supply seed stock. A Farmer can sow Cruise crops, and a Farmer or [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md) at the appropriate level can plant fruit-tree saplings. Pick fruit hanging under leaves. Fruit and produce feed the [Cook](cook.md) and [Chemist](chemist.md).

## Harvest quality and XP

Fine and Superb Harvest talents improve produce. Fine sells and pays XP at **1.25×**, Superb at **1.5×**. Both improve [dish quality](cook.md#cook-for-the-job-ahead). Crops pay full base XP through ten levels above their planting requirement, then lose **5% per level** to a floor of **25%**. Move to higher-level crops as they unlock. Check the profession book for your current bonus harvest chance.

## Combat techniques

Carry a hoe for each of these moves.

| Technique | Effect | Cooldown | Base damage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Reap | Sweeps enemies up to 4 blocks in front of you. | 9 s | 35 HP |
| Thorn Patch | Binds the target and enemies within 3 blocks for up to 2 seconds. | 12 s | 25 HP |
| Hay Maker | Launches a target within 4 blocks upward. | 10 s | 50 HP |

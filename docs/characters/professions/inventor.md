# Inventor

![](../../assets/playable/inocruise/gui/profession/inventor.png){ .entry-page-icon }

[All professions](index.md)

Set up the Workshop to make tools and supplies for other trades. Start with traps, rods, or instruments for your crew. Keep **Iron Scrap**, **Mysterious Parts**, **Pure Iron Ore**, **Diamond Fragments** and **Dial Fragments** for later jobs. The [Miner](miner.md) supplies ore and fragments, and expedition loot supplies parts.

## Workshop gear and upgrades

Use the **Upgrade Bench** to install compatible parts on rods, nets, traps or Cruise boats. A **Sieve** gathers Crystallized Salt from a water source in ocean or beach biomes. It supplies the [Cook](cook.md) and [Chemist](chemist.md). Build the [Lumberjack’s](lumberjack.md) Woodsman’s Axe or Felling Saw when the crew starts working special timbers.

The **Jet Dial** launches the user toward their aim. The **Burst Dial** fits a Cruise boat and burns Cola. The **Tone Dial** sounds when something approaches. The recipes below use Eternal Voyage’s requirements for these three dials.

### Use the Upgrade Bench

Craft the bench with three iron ingots across the top, planks on either side of a Smithing Table in the middle, and three planks across the bottom. Anyone can fit an upgrade. Put the base item in the left slot and the part in the middle. Read the preview before taking the result. Fit boat parts while the boat is an inventory item.

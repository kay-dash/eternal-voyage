# Carpenter

![](../../assets/playable/inocruise/gui/profession/carpenter.png){ .entry-page-icon }

[All professions](index.md)

Build Cruise boats at the Shipyard. The [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md) supplies specialist wood, the [Inventor](inventor.md) makes fittings, and the [Tailor](tailor.md) makes sails. These recipes produce ready-to-launch boats. Follow [Build and captain a ship](../../voyage/shipbuilding.md) to construct a ship block by block with an Eureka helm.

## Pick a hull and fit it out

| Hull | Shipyard levels | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reinforced | 1–13 | An accessible first boat using ordinary planks and iron. |
| Kuuigosu | 20–30 | Fast crossings. |
| Burning Tree | 36–40 | A fireproof hull that handles storms. |
| Adam | 50 | Maximum hull durability and protection in storms. |

Pick a normal boat or longboat for passengers, a cargo variant for storage, or a fishing variant for better catch odds. At the **Upgrade Bench**, fit armour plating for two extra blows, a ram, a sail for speed, or a **Burst Dial** for a Cola-powered boost. A Carpenter can mend a damaged Cruise boat afloat with planks, earning **2 XP per timber restored**. Keep repair supplies aboard.

## Outfit a port

Ship's Lockers, Reinforced Lockers, Kuuigosu Lockers and Adam Strongboxes hold **36, 55, 78 and 105 slots** respectively. The Adam Strongbox keeps its contents when picked up. Carpenters also make the Auction House, house and repopulation plans, and the fishing Record Board.

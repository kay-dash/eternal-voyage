# Musician

![](../../assets/playable/inocruise/gui/profession/musician.png){ .entry-page-icon }

[All professions](index.md)

Craft scores at the Music Stand and play them with an instrument from an [Inventor](inventor.md). Carry the instrument and use the score while your crew is close. Better instruments improve reach or duration. Writing scores earns the XP shown in the recipe table. Playing earns **10 + tune level**, plus **10 XP per crewmate who hears you**, for up to eight listeners. The Grand Piano lets you play at a fixed spot.

## Plan a performance

| Tune | Effect for the crew |
| --- | --- |
| Swift Shanty | Movement speed **+5%, +10%, +20%**. |
| Work Song | Block breaking speed **+10%, +20%, +40%**. |
| Lullaby of Mending | Heals half a heart every **12, 6, or 3 seconds**. |
| Sea Shanty | Swim speed **+25%, +50%, +100%**. |
| Battle March | Attack damage **+1, +2, +4**. |
| Lucky Tune | Luck **+0.5, +1, +2**. |
| Iron Chorus | Armour **+1.5, +3, +6** and toughness **+0.5, +1, +2**. |
| Leaping Jig | Jump boost **+0.05, +0.1, +0.2**. Negates the first **1.5, 3, or 6 blocks** of a fall. |
| Heartening Ballad | Absorption equal to **20%, 30%, or 40%** of maximum health. |
| Craftsman's Song | Gain **5%, 10%, or 20% trade XP** by tier. Stacks with Appetite. |
| Hymn of Resolve | Gain **5%, 10%, or 20% combat Doriki** by tier. Stacks with Fighting Spirit. |

Lost scores require a **Score Recipe** first. Read it as a Musician to learn the tune permanently. Moonlight Serenade gives night vision, Laboon’s Song lets listeners breathe underwater, Binks’ Sake shortens harmful effects and Drums of Liberation improves attack speed and knockback resistance.

## Combat techniques

Carry an instrument for these moves.

| Technique | Effect | Cooldown | Base damage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sour Note | Hits and knocks back enemies up to 7 blocks ahead. | 9 s | 25 HP |
| Reprise | Restarts your current tune for you and crewmates within instrument range. | 60 s | — |
| Dissonance | Hurts enemies within 5 blocks and weakens them for up to 5 seconds. | 12 s | 35 HP |

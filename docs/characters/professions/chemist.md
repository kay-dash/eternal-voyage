# Chemist

![](../../assets/playable/inocruise/gui/profession/chemist.png){ .entry-page-icon }

[All professions](index.md)

Set up a Laboratory and ask your [Farmer](farmer.md), [Hunter](hunter.md), [Fisher](fisher.md) and [Miner](miner.md) for ingredients. Start with Mix Candy for poison or Healing Candy for minor wounds. Later, make powders for your crew's recipes, potions for changing professions or talents, and thrown balls for fights. Bringing remedies to a village doctor during a fever also earns Chemist XP.

## Remedies and field supplies

You can take a remedy on a full hunger bar. Candies are quick to eat. Healing Tonic gives **5 hearts**, and Antidote Powder clears poison, wither, nausea and hunger. Stamina remedies restore food and remove some movement or exhaustion effects. The Restorative Drug heals **10 hearts** and clears several harmful effects.

Thrown battle balls burst on impact. Poison Ball inflicts Poison II for **6 seconds**, Sleep Ball slows, blinds and weakens for **8 seconds**, and Instant Freeze Ball freezes enemies and briefly puts thin ice on nearby still water. The **Potion of Second Thoughts** at level 20 refunds the talent points of one trade. The **Potion of Forgetting** at level 30 is for changing a chosen trade.

At level 36, make a **Revival Elixir**. Drinking it restores **20 hearts** and removes many ailments. Carrying one can consume it to save you from a fatal hit, restoring half your maximum health and briefly granting Regeneration and Absorption. A held Totem of Undying takes priority. **Medicine** at level 46 restores **15 hearts** and gives Regeneration III for **20 seconds**.

Thrown balls affect targets within **2.5 blocks** and have a half-second throw cooldown. **Salt Ball** slows for **4 seconds** and deals extra damage to animated shadows, undead, and water creatures.

A **Rumble Ball** lasts **three minutes**. It refreshes Devil Fruit cooldowns, boosts a transformed Zoan and lets a Mink enter Sulong without the full moon. Taking another while Rumble is active ends the effect and causes nausea, weakness and slowness.

## Combat techniques

Carry **Experiment Equipment** to use these techniques.

| Technique | Effect | Cooldown | Base damage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Acid Flask | Burns and poisons targets in a 3-block area for 4 seconds, halved for players and bosses. | 12 s | 25 HP |
| Field Dressing | Restores 10 HP and clears poison and wither. | 25 s | — |
| Smoke Screen | Blinds and slows enemies within 5 blocks for 6 seconds, halved for players and bosses. | 15 s | — |

# Survival at sea

## Weather and temperature

Tough As Nails has **temperature on** and **thirst off**. The companion adds island climates, weather effects, and temperature interactions for certain fruits, races, lineages, and traits. Lunarians are configured to avoid the hot extreme, while some cold-adapted lineages avoid the icy extreme.

The Blues have the mildest weather, while New World seas can see hurricane-strength cyclones. The Calm Belt remains windless. See [Weather and climate](weather.md) for forecasts, regional storm stages, ship handling, lining recipes, and racial and fruit temperature effects.

## Fruit users and bubbles

Sabaody’s coating yard sells a **Coated Bubble** for **5,000 Belly**. Its listed protection lasts **7.5 minutes**. A **Ryugu Bubble** from Ryugu Palace lasts **15 minutes**. Bubbly Coral is listed at **400 Belly** in the companion’s shop data. Kairoseki can pop the companion bubbles.

Reserve time and supplies for the return trip if you plan to take the underwater route. See [Devil Fruits and awakenings](../characters/devil-fruits.md#water-and-logia) for the water rules.

## Downed players and death

When you’re downed, a teammate within **three blocks** has about **60 seconds** to revive you. Reviving needs no item. Revives are unavailable in the Hide and Seek realm and Uta’s world.

If you die, you keep your race, faction, and fighting style. In areas with stat loss, you retain **77%** of your Doriki, Bounty, Belly, and Haki XP, and **50%** of your Loyalty. PvP can cost a [Devil Fruit life](../characters/devil-fruits.md#fruit-lives). Other players can open your corpse. [Impel Down](../voyage/impel-down.md) has separate arrest and confiscation rules.

# Loot and encounters

## Encounter scaling

The companion scales Mine Mine no Mi grunts, brutes, captains, and admirals by sea and role. Here are the four configured progression rows. Each pair lists base HP and NPC Doriki. Kits, abilities, or specific encounters can change the fight.

| Role | Blues | Early Paradise | Late Paradise | New World |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Grunt | 40 / 1,500 | 120 / 4,000 | 180 / 8,000 | 250 / 15,000 |
| Brute or sniper | 75 / 3,000 | 200 / 6,000 | 300 / 12,000 | 400 / 22,000 |
| Captain | 150 / 6,000 | 400 / 10,000 | 600 / 18,000 | 800 / 35,000 |
| Admiral | 300 / 8,000 | 700 / 16,000 | 1,000 / 25,000 | 1,400 / 48,000 |

Configured attack multipliers rise across those rows from **1× to 1.5×, 2×, and 3×**. See [Encounter stats](encounter-stats.md) for armor toughness, generic enemies, Cataclysm bosses, Mowzie’s mobs and Impel Down. Different ships and dungeons have their own crews. The **Victory Frigate** uses Marines, the **Unicorn Galleon** has government crews, and **Bandit Towers** have bandits.

Weak faction patrols have more frequent attempts at night, in configured groups of three to six. The natural spawn mix targets **49% Mine Mine no Mi**, **21% aquatic family**, and **30% vanilla** within a 96-block sample. These ratios govern managed natural spawns. Commands, spawn eggs, breeding and named or persistent mobs are outside that balance. Mary Geoise’s Red Line has a separate setting favoring Celestial Dragons.

## Ruins and sea dungeons

One Piece World places dungeons by sea and island type. Check the Log Pose for the local ruins mission. Some missions count defenders inside the structure, while others ask for the boss.

| Region | Example location | Log Pose objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blues | Abandoned Temple | Defeat eight defenders inside. |
| Blues | Infested Temple on eligible jungle ruins | Defeat twelve defenders inside. |
| Blues | Scorched Mines in desert or badlands ruins | Defeat ten defenders inside. |
| Blues | Plague Asylum on winter ruins | Defeat twelve defenders inside. |
| Early Paradise | Cursed Pyramid | Defeat the Ancient Remnant. |
| Late Paradise | Frosted Prison | Defeat Maledictus. |
| New World | Ancient Factory | Defeat the Harbinger. |

One Piece World’s sea rules also place the Sunken City in eligible deep ocean in late Paradise and the New World. The Acropolis is assigned to warm ocean in the New World.

## Return to a dungeon

Eligible structures can recover after **96 real hours** without activity there. Active areas and changes to the structure can keep it from resetting. Leave dungeons you want to revisit as dungeons and make your base elsewhere.

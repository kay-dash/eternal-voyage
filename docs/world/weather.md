# Weather and climate

Weather moves across the seas. Rain, wind, or thunder on the next island may never reach your ship. Check a forecast before you leave and check conditions again before using a move that depends on a storm.

## Storms by sea

| Waters | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| The Blues | Thunderstorms and gales. This is the gentlest stretch for learning to sail. |
| Early Paradise | Hail, weaker cyclones and tornadoes, and the first natural snowstorms and sandstorms. |
| Late Paradise and the Red Line | Stronger cyclones and tornadoes and more frequent rough weather. |
| New World | The strongest storms, including hurricanes and open-sea snowstorms. |
| Calm Belt | Clear and windless. Plan around [Sea Kings](loot.md#sea-kings) and the lack of wind. |

## Read the weather and protect your ship

- **Barometer.** Check the direction and approximate distance of a storm, whether it’s coming toward you, and how strong it could become. If you’re already in it, check whether it’s getting stronger or weakening.
- **Navigator’s Eye.** A Navigator can get warnings as a nearby storm strengthens. Use the warning to turn away or make landfall before the worst of it.
- **Ships and fishing.** Storms can strike and batter ships, especially weaker hulls. Ask a Carpenter for hull plating, which lets your ship take two more blows. Some fish are easier to catch during storms.
- **Tornadoes and cyclones.** They can lift you, mobs, light blocks, and debris. Give the funnel room to pass and seek shelter from hail, flying debris, and falls.

## Use local weather in combat

Weather-sensitive attacks check conditions where they matter. If a move calls for thunder over you, a storm over the next island will not help.

| Local weather | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Thunder over you | Enables storm checks for moves and forms such as Raigo, Lightning Reign, and Storm Sovereign, plus some cursed and black weapon effects. Eligible Thunderbolt, Thunderstorm, Thunder Lance and Supercell hits deal **1.25×** damage. |
| Thunder over the target | Matters for lightning effects on thrown weapons, including Coral Spear and Coral Bardiche. |
| Cloud cover, rain and thunder | Affect the light needed by Photosynthesis and the Vampire fruit’s night check. Residents can head indoors during thunder. Rain can chill you. |

Rain Tempo and Weatheria services can call local rain. Thunderstorm Tempo, Raigo, and Seiryu’s storm technique can create **60 seconds** of local thunder. Fubuki creates a **20-second** snowstorm and Grande Sables creates a **15-second** sandstorm. Expect Scylla to call rain or thunder during its fight.

## Island climate and body temperature

Pack for the island you’re approaching. Jungle islands can have local showers and volcanic islands can have ash. The open sea is generally neutral and the Calm Belt is warm. Desert and volcanic islands tend to be hot, while winter islands are cold or icy. Snow, wet clothes, elevation, and nearby fire can change how quickly your temperature moves. Watch the temperature indicator and find shelter or equip lined armor before extremes start to hurt.

### Fruits, races and traits

The companion recognizes **Mera Mera, Magu Magu, Netsu Netsu, Gutsu Gutsu, and Atsu Atsu** as heat fruits, and **Hie Hie and Yuki Yuki** as cold fruits. Its configured aura range is **16 blocks**. Lunarians and the configured Lunarian lineage are protected against the hot extreme. Polar Bear Mink race or lineage, the listed wool and snow lineages, and Warm Blood protect against the icy extreme. **Chilly** makes cold harsher and **Dry Skin** makes heat harsher.
